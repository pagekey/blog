What can Tailscale do for you? This video is all about explaining the what, why, and how of Tailscale, a VPN unlike any other, designed to connect all of your devices easily and (depending on your needs) for free!

{% youtube "iY6t491p3yY" %}

This is a major win for anyone who wants to self-host services for their personal use, but doesn't want to pay a ton of money for hosting, nor expose themselves to hackers by exposing their service on the open internet.
