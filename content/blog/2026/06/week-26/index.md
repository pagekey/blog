## 4. Freeform / Ramble

Over the last few weeks, I showed what’s possible when you own your hardware—replacing Google Photos and automating your own backups. But I realized something: starting with containers and backup scripts is like building the roof before pouring the concrete.

People want to escape the cloud, but the biggest barrier isn't the software. It's the hardware. It's taking an old, discarded computer and actually turning it into a reliable server. 

So this week, I went back to step zero. I kicked off the "Escape the Cloud" series with a dedicated guide on installing Debian. No graphical desktop, no bloat. Just a lightweight, headless server that you can manage from your laptop while it sits quietly on a shelf in another room.

This is the foundation. Before you can host your own cloud, before you can replace your subscriptions, you need a server you actually control. And the best part? You don't need a thousand-dollar rack mount. That old laptop gathering dust is exactly where you start.

The mission hasn't changed. We are taking back tech. And it starts with the foundation.
